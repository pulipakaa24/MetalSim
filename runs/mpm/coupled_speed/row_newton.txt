Metal: in the rheology loop, a rigidity product whose rows `warp.sparse.bsr_mv` would sum with its tiled kernel goes through `bsr_mv` itself instead of the listed-rows kernel. These are the body rows of IJtm: one block per collider node of the body. With the Warp fork's `metal_bsr_mv_tiles`, that is CUDA's kernel and summation order. The J product, with one row per grid node, keeps the listed rows. `example_mpm_twoway_coupling`: IJtm (12 rows, up to about 1,170 blocks each) went from 29-40 to 12.1-12.9 ms per 50 calls. Late in the run, the GPU kernel sum per frame went from 55.3 to 36.4 ms. Without that Warp option, the product is unchanged (listed rows, bitwise as before).
