### Realism, CPU, every 5 ms step, 100 episodes (`port/realism.json`)

Servo specs:

- SO-ARM100 README (BOM): the follower uses 6× STS3215 **7.4 V C001** (1/345). A 12 V option has 30 kg·cm stall
  torque. <https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100>
- Feetech datasheet: C001 is 19.5 kg·cm = 1.91 N·m at 7.4 V, 52 rpm = 5.45 rad/s.
  <https://www.mantech.co.za/datasheets/products/sts3215-200620a-1.pdf>
- C018, 12 V: 2.94 N·m, 0.222 s/60° = 4.72 rad/s (vendor listings: robotshop.com, eckstein-shop.de).
- Model (`assets/so101/so101.xml:31-38`): kp 998.22, kv 2.731, forcerange 2.94, damping 0.6. That gives a motor line
  with a 4.9 rad/s no-load speed, so the model is the 12 V servo.
- LeRobot caps the gripper at Max_Torque_Limit 500 (50 %) and Overload_Torque 25 % (`so_follower.py` `configure`).
  <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huggingface/lerobot/main/src/lerobot/robots/so_follower/so_follower.py>

| joint | peak rad/s | at 2.94 N·m | outside 12 V bound | outside 7.4 V bound (p99) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pan | 5.01 | 99.7 % | 2.5 % | 99.4 % (1.84) |
| lift | 5.14 | 98.4 % | 3.8 % | 98.1 % (1.83) |
| elbow | 5.40 | 99.8 % | 3.5 % | 98.6 % (1.80) |
| wrist flex | 5.18 | 19.6 % | 6.5 % | 16.3 % (1.76) |
| wrist roll | 4.99 | 100 % | 6.5 % | 96.2 % (1.85) |
| gripper | 5.65 | 100 % | 6.7 % | 96.4 % (1.67) |

- The bound is the four-quadrant DC limit |τ/τs + ω/ω0| ≤ 1, with τ the servo force minus damping, counted at 2 %
  tolerance.
- Speeds are plausible: above 4.72 rad/s on ≤ 1.4 % of steps, and above 5.45 rad/s once.
- **The policy is bang-bang:**
  - 80 % of actions sit at ±1;
  - the median command jump is 1.96 rad per 20 ms;
  - during the hold, arm commands flip on 68 % of steps (close to a 25 Hz square wave);
  - during the hold, the joints move at 0.87 rad/s median.
- **Gripper:**
  - squeezes at the full 2.94 N·m for the whole hold, 106 N summed normal force (median);
  - 12 jaw contacts (median), with no floor or other-link contact;
  - the box lies about on its side, 84° median tilt;
  - largest penetration is 3.2 mm median and 10.6 mm max.
- **Lift:** box peak vertical speed 0.86 m/s (median), about 2 g.
- **Drop:** seed 10014 counts as a success but drops the box later.
- **Verdict:** unrealistic for the standard 7.4 V follower. The motion fits a 12 V arm only by running every servo
  flat out, with full-range command switching and twice LeRobot's gripper torque.
