## Verified

### Sources for the servo model

- **12 V values.** Scene `assets/so101/so101.xml:31-38`: kp 998.22, kv 2.731 and forcerange 2.94 come from Gregory119's
  `calc_actuator_model.py`, written for the 12 V ST-3215-C047 (30 kg·cm, 45 rpm, Gp 16). They were merged in
  SO-ARM100 PR #141, which replaced kp 17.8 and forcerange 3.35.
- **Damping, frictionloss, armature.** Damping 0.60, frictionloss 0.052 and armature 0.028 date from SO-ARM100's first
  SO-101 file. They match Rhoban BAM's `to_mujoco.py` export of `params/feetech_sts3215_7_4V/m1.json`:
  - damping = viscous + kt²/R = 0.62;
  - Coulomb friction 0.0515;
  - armature 0.026.

  This origin is inferred from the matching numbers only.
- **No speed-dependent limit in the actuator.** MuJoCo clamps the whole servo force at the force limit. The flat-out
  line comes from the joint damping alone. So that the line ends at the datasheet's 5.45 rad/s, damping becomes
  1.912/5.445.
- **Datasheet** (`upstream/sts3215_200620a.txt`):
  - 19.5 kg·cm and 52 rpm at 7.4 V;
  - torque output is the voltage duty cycle;
  - no stall or no-load current, so kp and kv are kept.

### Commands

- **CPU:** so101Sim venv, MuJoCo 3.10.0. `reference_servo.py ... 7v4 100`, run plain, then with `--keep-damping`, then
  with `--gripper-full`.
  - Sensitivity, damping kept at 0.60: 76 success, 74 up at 6 s.
  - Diagnostic, gripper at full torque: 98 success, 96 up at 6 s.
- **MetalSim**, GPU queue `so101-74v-compare`: `compare_servo.py` for 7v4 and 12v.
  - 7.4 V: 81/100 seeds have the same outcome as on CPU. Starts match to 3e-8.
  - Solver caps: ITERATIONS in 16 worlds, line search in 100.
- **Torque at the limit** (12 V → 7.4 V, CPU):
  - pan 99.7 → 90.8 %;
  - lift 98.4 → 96.9 %;
  - elbow 99.8 → 99.9 %;
  - wrist flex 19.6 → 53.4 %;
  - roll 100 → 100 %;
  - gripper 100 → 98.6 %.

  MetalSim gives the same within 2 %.
- **Failure mode (CPU):**
  - when the jaws first touch the box, the gripper is open 0.47 rad, against 0.90 rad at 12 V;
  - the box tips past 60° on the table in 76 episodes, against 43 at 12 V;
  - in 21 episodes the box is never above 6 cm: it lies on its side, and the policy hovers with the gripper open;
  - during the hold the jaws squeeze with 27 N, against 106 N; 8 boxes drop (13 on MetalSim).
- **Film**, GPU queue `so101-74v-film`: `python -m metalsim.render.film.so101 so101_lift_74v`, `--stage record`, then
  `--stage render`.
  - The stamps say AC power, charging, 97–98 %, powermode 2, 15:17–15:19.
  - 12 of 16 worlds succeed.
  - Then `--stage encode --shots hero`, and the WebP made with so101_lift's exact command and window.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_film.py` and `tests/test_no_machine_paths.py`: 5 passed, 1 skipped (newton, already skipped
  before). They ran outside the queue while it was idle.
